Market Radar

Market Radar is a personal app for following news, results dates and events of listed companies. It runs only on its owner's own computer and is not offered to the public.

If its owner chooses, Market Radar can copy upcoming company dates (results, meetings, dividends and similar) into a separate calendar called "Market Radar" in the owner's own Google Calendar.
