Privacy policy

Market Radar · Last updated 14 November 2026

Who uses this app

Market Radar is a personal app. It runs only on its owner's own Windows computer and has a single user: its owner. It has no servers, no user accounts and no other users.

What Google data it uses

Market Radar asks Google for one permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created. With it, the app can create one calendar called "Market Radar" in the owner's Google account and add, change or delete dates in that calendar. It cannot see or change any other calendar or event.

It writes company dates (for example results days, shareholder meetings and dividend dates) into that calendar. It does not read the owner's email, contacts, files or other Google data.

Where the data is kept

  • The sign-in key Google gives the app is stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted with Windows for that user account.
  • The calendar dates live in the owner's own Google Calendar and on the owner's computer.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Market Radar talks only to Google's own servers.

What it does not do

  • It does not sell, share or transfer Google data to anyone.
  • It does not use Google data for advertising.
  • It does not use Google data to train AI or machine-learning models.

Market Radar's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping and removing data

In Market Radar, Settings › Calendar › Disconnect removes the sign-in key from the computer, cancels the app's access at Google and can delete the "Market Radar" calendar. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on Market Radar's Google sign-in screen.
